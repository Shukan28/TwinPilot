Accenture Challenge PS #4 Concept

The Challenge: Responsibly design, deploy, and scale digital twins of manufacturing assemblies that move beyond basic dashboards towards predictive counters, simulated interventions, and explainable AI interfaces.

Our Solution (TwinPilot): We build an intelligent twin that:

  • Predicts Anomalies: Flags bottleneck events before they disrupt throughput.
  • Infers Sensorless Zones: Models state estimation for areas with zero physical sensors (e.g. S8).
  • Simulates Interventions: Evaluates Option A, B, and C in real-time, outputting dynamic throughput, defect rates, and business losses.
  • Brings Human-in-the-Loop: Implements a natural language dialog interface and logs override audits.
